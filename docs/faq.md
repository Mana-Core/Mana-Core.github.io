# FAQ

## What is Mana Core?

A GitHub organization and index for 3rd Party **Mana** extensions. It lists extension repositories, documents how to build them, and gives abandoned repositories a home where others can keep them working.

## Is Mana Core part of Mana?

No. Mana Core is 3rd Party-run. Issues with the app itself belong with the Mana developers; issues with a source belong in that source's repository.

## A source stopped working. What now?

Sites change their layout often. Open the repository page, press **Report an issue**, and include the source name, its version and what fails. Pull requests with a fix are even better.

## Why don't I see NSFW sources?

They're hidden by default. Open the settings menu (<span class="mc-icon i-settings"></span>) in the top bar and turn on **NSFW Sources**.

## How often is the index updated?

The site rebuilds every day and on every change to this repository, so new sources and versions appear within 24 hours.

## How do I change the site's colors?

Open the settings menu (<span class="mc-icon i-settings"></span>) and pick an **Accent Color**. **Accent Background** tints the whole page, and **Effects** toggles animations. Your choices are saved in your browser.
